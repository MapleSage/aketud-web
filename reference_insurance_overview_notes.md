# Insurance reference findings

The public reference at `https://www.maplesage.com/insurane` frames an insurance vertical around AI-powered operations from claims intake through underwriting. It highlights claims automation and routing, underwriting risk scoring, real-time fraud detection, and customer portal integration. It also positions the outcome as faster claim handling and improved fraud detection.

For AKETUD, these themes will be re-authored around the existing platform reality rather than copied: evidence-grounded intake, underwriting workbench, rating and policy issuance, premium accounting, claims triage, agency operations, and the shared policy record. Platform remains a separate product page; the new Insurance page will be the service and operating-capability overview that connects the products.

## Route audit

The current public service routes are structurally thin: each is a **13–15 line wrapper** around generic hero, feature, and CTA components. Their content records have approximately **1.2–1.7 KB** of copy and no media references. The homepage is a **37 line** composition with no page-owned media references. The product template is richer, using existing operating-system screenshots, but the service routes do not reach that level of visual proof. The rebuild will use those authentic product-screen assets, the supplied application montage, and carefully chosen operational photography in distinct page layouts rather than repeat a single dark-blue service pattern.

## Public-image correction review

The current public reference uses illustrative, generated operational imagery rather than internal product screenshots as its service-page visual language. Its insurance graphic contains legacy SageInsure and HubSpot labels; its AI automation graphic contains SageCMO, SageRetail, and SageInsure labels. These images can inform the composition and tonal treatment, but they should not be placed unmodified in AKETUD public pages because they would expose obsolete product and partner branding. The correction will therefore remove the internal screen imagery and keep a restrained public graphic treatment based on the reference’s dark operations-room imagery, without surfacing those legacy labels.

## Correction verification
