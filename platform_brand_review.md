# Platform Page Brand Review

## Verified integration

The live Platform route carries the supplied full AKETUD lockup in the shared header and the inverse full lockup in the updated tonal-teal footer. The page’s title, metadata and calls to action are Platform-specific, and the footer is visually consistent with the homepage treatment.

## Gap to resolve

**“AI You Can Accept” is not currently present on the Platform page.** The main homepage carries the line, but the Platform route begins directly with “Platform” and “Operations Command Center.” That is defensible for a product page, but it leaves the brand promise disconnected from the product proof.

## Recommended low-impact treatment

Keep the Platform headline as **Platform**. Add a restrained brand line above its existing eyebrow or as a one-line label beneath the header:

> **AKETUD / AI YOU CAN ACCEPT**

This retains a product-first hero while connecting the Platform to the main brand promise. Do not repeat the full “AI You Can Accept” display treatment from the homepage, where it should remain the main brand statement.

No implementation change was made during this review.
